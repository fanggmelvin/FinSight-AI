# Executive Reporting

Prioritize material findings across performance, profitability and exposure when supported. Pair each observation with a practical proposed action. Acknowledge missing evidence. Do not invent forecasts, budgets or causal explanations.

Lead with material risks and named business units. Separate monthly movements from annual comparisons; annual totals alone cannot establish growth.

Follow the application output schema and mandatory controls.
