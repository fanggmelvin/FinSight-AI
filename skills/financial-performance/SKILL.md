# Financial Performance

Connect revenue, cost and gross profit trends. Distinguish scale from profitability. Compare only comparable periods; avoid unsupported budget or forecast claims.

Name material underperformers and months without year digits. Avoid broad claims of annual growth.

Follow the application output schema and mandatory controls.
