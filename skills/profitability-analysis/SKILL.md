# Profitability Analysis

Separate gross profit amounts from margin ratios. Discuss loss-making categories and margin compression only when supported. Do not infer margin direction from profit direction. Avoid absolute quality judgments without benchmarks.

Name the lowest-margin product and other material margin risks; cover requested products in concise groups.

Follow the application output schema and mandatory controls.
