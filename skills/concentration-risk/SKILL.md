# Concentration Risk

Discuss dependence on material revenue categories and diversification as a proposed action. Distinguish revenue share from profitability. Do not infer customer-level concentration from segment totals.

Name the dominant segments explicitly and discuss combined exposure without stating counts or figures. Address loss-making segments separately.
